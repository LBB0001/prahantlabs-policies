## Deleting Files — Read This Part

AI Phone Cleaner helps you find files that are usually safe to remove. **You choose
what is deleted, and deletion is permanent.**

- Nothing is removed unless you select it and confirm.
- The app has no recycle bin and cannot restore a deleted file. Neither can we.
- Where Android requires its own confirmation, that dialog is the final step;

- Keep a backup of anything you would be sorry to lose. This is ordinary advice
  for any tool that deletes files, and we mean it.

The app marks byte-for-byte identical copies as safe, and always keeps one copy
of each. Every other suggestion — near-identical photos, screenshots, large
videos, downloads — is a suggestion. Look before you confirm.

## What The App Cannot Do

We would rather say this plainly than let the store description imply
otherwise:

- **It cannot clear other apps' caches.** Android has not permitted that since
  Android 6. The app measures cache per app and can open each app's own
  settings page, where you clear it yourself. It clears only its own cache
  directly.
- **It is not an antivirus.** The Security screen checks where apps were
  installed from, which sensitive permissions they hold, whether any are debug
  builds, and whether your screen is locked. It does not detect malware and
  makes no claim to.
- **It does not report a battery health percentage.** Android does not tell
  apps how much capacity a battery has lost, so no app can calculate that
  honestly. We show the health state, temperature, voltage and charge cycles
  the system does report.
- **It cannot uninstall apps by itself.** It selects them; Android asks you to
  confirm each one.
