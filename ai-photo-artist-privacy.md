# Privacy Policy — AI Photo Artist - Art Generator

**Last updated 21 August 2026**

AI Photo Artist - Art Generator is developed by PrahantLabs. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and photos.

## What Data We Collect & How It Is Used

### 1. Photos and Images
- **Photo Processing:** When you upload or select a photo to edit or transform into AI art, the image is used solely to generate your requested art styles, photo enhancements, and AI transformations.
- **No Permanent Storage of Personal Photos:** Your original and generated photos are stored locally on your device. Images processed for AI art generation are handled in real-time and are not stored permanently on our servers or used to train public AI models.

### 2. Usage Data & Analytics
The app uses standard measurement and advertising analytics (such as Google Analytics for Firebase and Meta SDKs) to improve app performance and user experience:
- Anonymous device details (device model, OS version, country/region).
- App feature usage statistics (which tools or art styles were selected).
- Crash diagnostics and performance logs.

### 3. Personal Data
- We do **not** require user registration or personal login accounts.
- We do **not** collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, precise location, or personal identity details.

## Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data or photos to third parties. We integrate standard third-party developer services:
- **Meta SDK & Meta Audience Network:** For ad delivery, ad performance measurement, and Meta app analytics.
- **Google Play Services & Firebase:** For performance tracking, analytics, and diagnostic reporting.

Both services operate in accordance with their respective privacy policies ([Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## User Data Deletion & Storage Control

- **Deleting Generated Photos:** You can delete any saved or generated artwork directly from your device's photo gallery at any time.
- **Data Deletion Requests:** Because we do not store user accounts or personal profiles on remote servers, no personal profile data exists to delete. For any privacy or diagnostic data questions, contact us at **prahantlabs@gmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
