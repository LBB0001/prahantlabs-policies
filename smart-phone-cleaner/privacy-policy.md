# Privacy Policy — Smart Phone Cleaner

**Last updated 1 September 2026**

Smart Phone Cleaner is developed by PrahantLabs. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: there is no account, every scan runs
on your phone, no file or file name ever leaves the device, and the app
measures how it is used so we know what to improve.

## What The App Reads

Smart Phone Cleaner looks at your storage to find files worth removing. All of that
reading happens on the device. Nothing found by a scan is uploaded, copied off
the phone, or shared with anyone.

Specifically, the app reads:

- **Photos and videos** — file name, size, date and format, so it can group
  duplicates, near-identical shots and oversized media. To compare photos it
  also reads the images themselves, on the device, and reduces each to a
  64-bit fingerprint. The photos are never transmitted and the fingerprints are
  discarded when the scan ends.
- **Folders you choose** — if you grant a folder through Android's document
  picker, the app lists the files inside it so downloads and large documents
  can be checked. The grant covers only the folder you picked, and you can
  withdraw it in Settings.
- **Installed apps** — if you grant Usage Access, the app reads each app's
  size, cache size and when it was last opened, so it can show which apps take
  the most space and which you no longer use. This is read on the device and
  never transmitted.

## What We Collect

Smart Phone Cleaner has no account, no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name,
email address or phone number, and we have no way to identify you personally.

The app uses these Google services:

**Google Analytics for Firebase** — records which screens you open and which
actions are used: starting a scan, opening a category, confirming a clean-up.
It also records how much space a clean-up freed, as a number. This is how we
learn which parts of the app are worth improving. It is read as counts across
all users, never as a profile of one.

**Firebase Crashlytics** — if the app crashes, it reports the error and the
screen it happened on, so the fault can be found and fixed.

**Google AdMob** — the app shows ads. AdMob receives your device's advertising
identifier along with standard technical detail, and uses it to select and
measure ads. You can reset or delete that identifier at any time in Android
Settings, under Privacy or Ads.

All three also collect standard technical detail: a randomly generated
installation identifier, device model, operating-system version, country, and
app version. That identifier is not your Google account, and it is discarded
when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

We do **not** collect your file names, photos, videos, documents, the list of
apps installed on your phone, or the contents of any scan. We do not sell data.

Google processes this data on our behalf; its own handling is described at
[policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and, for
advertising, at
[policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## What Stays On Your Device

The app saves a small amount of state locally so it behaves sensibly between
sessions:

- how much space each clean-up freed, and when
- your language, theme and notification choices
- which folders you granted access to
- the result of your most recent scan, until the next one

This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions, And Why

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| Photos and videos | To find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large media. Without it those categories cannot be checked at all. |
| Notifications | To tell you when storage is nearly full or clutter has measurably grown. Optional; the app works without it. |
| Usage Access | To show app sizes, cache sizes and how recently each app was used. Optional, and granted only from Android's own settings screen. |
| Query all packages | To list installed apps for the App Storage screen. Used for nothing else. |
| Folder access | Granted per folder through Android's document picker, so downloads and documents can be checked. Optional and revocable. |
| Internet | To load ads and send crash reports. No scan data is sent over it. |

## Deleting Files

Nothing is deleted unless you select it and confirm. For files the app does not
own, Android shows its own confirmation as well. Deletion is permanent — the
app has no recycle bin and cannot recover a removed file. Please see the
[Terms of Use](../terms/) on this point.

## Turning Measurement Off

Notifications can be turned off in the app's Settings, or in Android's own
notification settings. Ad personalisation can be limited by resetting or
deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings.

There is no in-app switch for analytics and crash reporting today.
Uninstalling Smart Phone Cleaner stops all collection and discards the installation
identifier. If you would like an in-app control, write to us — it is a fair
thing to ask for.

## Children

Smart Phone Cleaner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information,
write to us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights

Because we hold no account and cannot identify you, we hold no personal file we
could send you or delete on request. To remove everything associated with your
installation, uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our lawful basis for analytics and crash reporting
is legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it; for
advertising it is consent, collected in the app where required.
