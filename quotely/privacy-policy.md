# Privacy Policy — Quotely

**Last updated 31 August 2026**

Quotely is developed by PrahantLabs. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** Quotely has no account, no sign-up and no login. It sends no data
to us or to anyone else. We have no server that stores your information and no
way to identify you.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

## What Stays On Your Device

The app saves a small amount of state locally so it behaves sensibly between
sessions:

- the language you chose
- the cards you saved
- your card style choices
- whether the daily reminder is on, and the time you picked

This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions

**Notifications** — used only for the optional daily verse, and only if you
allow it. If you decline, the app works exactly the same without the reminder.
You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Quotely requests no other permissions. It does not access your contacts,
location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Sharing a Card

When you share a card, the app writes the image to your device's temporary
folder and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is
entirely your choice — we are not involved and cannot see it.

## Internet Use

All quotes and shayari are bundled inside the app and work offline. The only
network request the app can make is to download display fonts once, from Google
Fonts, which are then cached on your device. No personal data is sent with that
request.

## Children

Quotely is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with
a new date at the top.

## Contact
