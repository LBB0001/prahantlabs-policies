## Using The App

## The Words In The App

Quotely ships two libraries:

- **Quotes** — written originally for this app. You may share these cards
  anywhere, including commercially, without asking us.
- **Shayari** — a mix of original modern verse and work by classical poets.
  Every attributed verse is in the public domain, and the poet is named on the
  card. Work by living poets is deliberately excluded.

We have taken care with attribution. If you believe a verse is misattributed or
should not be included, contact us and we will correct or remove it.

## Sharing Cards

credits a poet — it is the least the verse is owed.
